Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 5, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to MathBoard. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we handle information when you use our interactive digital whiteboard application.

2. Information We Collect

MathBoard is designed with privacy in mind. We collect minimal information:

2.1 Information Stored Locally

  • Drawing Board State: Your drawings and formulas are stored locally in your browser's storage
  • User Preferences: Settings like zoom level, tool selection, and UI preferences
  • Cookie Consent: Your cookie policy preferences
Important: All your work is stored locally on your device. We do not have access to your drawings, formulas, or any content you create with MathBoard.

2.2 Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Personal identification information (name, email, address)
  • IP addresses or device identifiers
  • Usage analytics or tracking data
  • Your mathematical content or drawings

3. Cookies and Local Storage

MathBoard uses browser storage technologies for essential functionality:

3.1 Essential Cookies

  • Session Storage: Temporarily stores your active drawing board state
  • Local Storage: Saves your preferences and cookie consent choices

3.2 What We Don't Use

  • Third-party tracking cookies
  • Analytics cookies
  • Advertising cookies
  • Social media cookies

4. How We Use Your Information

The information stored locally on your device is used solely to:

  • Provide the core functionality of MathBoard
  • Remember your preferences and settings
  • Save your work between sessions
  • Improve your user experience

5. Data Security

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you maintain complete control over your information. We recommend:

  • Regularly backing up your work by exporting it
  • Using a secure browser with up-to-date security patches
  • Being cautious when using shared or public computers

6. Data Retention and Deletion

You can delete your data at any time by:

  • Clearing your browser's local storage
  • Clearing your browser's cache and cookies
  • Using your browser's privacy tools

7. Third-Party Services

MathBoard may contain links to external websites (such as donation platforms). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third-party sites. We encourage you to read their privacy policies.

8. Children's Privacy

MathBoard is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children or any other users. Parents and educators can use MathBoard with confidence knowing that no personal data is collected or transmitted.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

10. GDPR Compliance

For users in the European Union, MathBoard complies with GDPR requirements. Since we do not collect or process personal data on servers, most GDPR obligations do not apply. However:

  • You have full control over data stored locally on your device
  • You can delete all data at any time through your browser settings
  • We do not share data with third parties
  • We do not perform any profiling or automated decision-making

11. California Privacy Rights

For California residents, under the CCPA: We do not sell your personal information. We do not collect personal information beyond what is stored locally on your device.
