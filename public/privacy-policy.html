Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

1. Introduction and Controller

MathBoard is a free, open-source mathematics whiteboard available at mathboard.app and as an installed desktop application. This notice explains which information stays on your device and which technical information may be processed when you visit the website, use product analytics, or contact the project.

For privacy questions and requests, the project maintainer and contact is Giacomo Bartoloni. Contact details appear in section 12.

2. Information and Data Flows

2.1 Data stored on your device

  • Board content: Drawings, formulas, text and saved boards are stored in browser or desktop WebView storage, including IndexedDB; active session state may also use session storage.
  • Preferences: Appearance and other editor settings may be stored in local storage.
Board privacy: MathBoard does not send your board content, drawings, entered text or LaTeX as analytics metadata. Sharing an exported file or a board link is a separate action you initiate.

2.2 Website analytics

On mathboard.app, MathBoard uses Simple Analytics to count page views and categorical product events, such as tools being selected or operations completed. Event metadata is limited by MathBoard's product policy to functional categories and counts, plus values such as runtime and app_version. It does not intentionally include board content, user IDs or persistent device identifiers.

The provider can receive page URLs, referrers and campaign parameters and processes ordinary connection information when requests arrive, including the IP address in transit. It states that it discards IP addresses rather than storing them and does not set analytics cookies or persistent browser identifiers. MathBoard configures the browser script to omit optional country/time-zone, language, user-agent, screen/viewport size, page-load identifier, time-on-page and scroll metrics. See the provider's metrics documentation and privacy policy.

2.3 Installed desktop analytics

Production desktop builds send counts of approved semantic product events through a native HTTPS transport to Simple Analytics. They do not send page views. Events carry approved functional categories, runtime=desktop and the app version, without board content, URLs, user identifiers or device identifiers. The provider necessarily receives technical connection data for each request.

Desktop analytics drops events while offline or when the WebView exposes a Do Not Track value of 1 or yes. Some WebViews may not expose an operating-system Do Not Track setting. Events are not stored persistently or retried. Development builds do not send analytics.

2.4 Website delivery and contact

The public website is served through Cloudflare. To deliver the website and protect its infrastructure, Cloudflare may process request information such as IP addresses, requested resources, timestamps, user-agent and security-related metadata. See the Cloudflare Privacy Policy. If you email the project, the message and your contact details are processed to handle your enquiry.

2.5 What is not part of analytics

MathBoard does not use analytics for advertising profiles or cross-site identification, and does not intentionally transmit user-entered board content, email addresses or free-text fields in product events. This does not eliminate the technical request data processed by the hosting and analytics providers.

3. Cookies and On-Device Storage

MathBoard uses local browser or WebView storage to keep the editor usable, preserve saved boards and remember preferences. This application storage is separate from measurement of website visits.

MathBoard does not use advertising or profiling cookies. Its standard Simple Analytics integration does not place analytics cookies, use persistent browser identifiers or read a stored cookie-consent choice. No consent banner is presented for the currently configured, cookieless aggregate measurements. If non-exempt tracking is added in the future, its consent requirements must be reassessed before deployment.

4. Purposes and Legal Bases

Where personal data is processed, the purposes and legal bases are:

  • Website delivery and security: Technical request and security information may be processed to provide a reliable service and protect against abuse, based on legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Project support: When you voluntarily send an email, its contents and contact details are used to respond to you, based on legitimate interests in handling correspondence (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Product statistics: Simple Analytics is configured to produce privacy-focused pageview and product-event statistics without user or device identifiers. To the extent any incidental personal data is processed in delivering these measurements, the purpose is improving MathBoard through aggregate usage insights, based on legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR), subject to any applicable ePrivacy requirements.

Editor content and preferences normally remain on your device, rather than being received by MathBoard as server-side data. MathBoard does not sell personal data or use the analytics described here for behavioural advertising.

5. Data Security

Board content and preferences are stored locally on your device, so you retain control over that information. We recommend:

  • Regularly backing up your work by exporting it
  • Using a secure browser with up-to-date security patches
  • Being cautious when using shared or public computers

6. Retention and Deletion

Boards and preferences stored locally remain on your device until you delete them, clear the applicable browser or WebView storage, or uninstall the application (subject to your device's storage behaviour). You can also remove individual saved boards using MathBoard's local board controls.

Analytics reporting data is retained according to the Simple Analytics account plan and the provider's documented retention rules. The free plan currently offers one month of analytics history; other plans can differ. See Simple Analytics plans and retention details. Operational/security information processed by Cloudflare is subject to its product and account settings and privacy documentation.

Email enquiries are retained as necessary to respond and for any applicable legal obligations. You can request deletion of personal data held by the project where the right applies by using the contact address below.

7. Providers and External Services

MathBoard uses Cloudflare to deliver the public site and Simple Analytics for the limited measurement described in sections 2.2 and 2.3. These providers have their own published privacy practices and may use subprocessors. Simple Analytics states that its standard analytics infrastructure is based in the EU; Cloudflare's operations and relevant international-transfer safeguards are described in its privacy notice. No optional transfer-dependent Simple Analytics dashboard feature is required for MathBoard's basic analytics.

MathBoard may link to external sites (including donation platforms and GitHub). Visiting those sites is a separate interaction governed by their privacy notices.

8. Children's Privacy

MathBoard is an educational tool that can be used by children. It does not require an account or ask children to provide identifying information to draw or edit a board. Website traffic and approved aggregate product events may still be measured as described above; they are not designed to identify individual children. Any direct email correspondence is handled as described in section 2.4.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

10. GDPR Rights

Where the GDPR applies to personal data processed in connection with MathBoard, you may have the rights to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, and, where its conditions are met, data portability. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests. These rights depend on the processing and applicable legal requirements; aggregate, non-identifying analytics may not allow individual records to be located.

To exercise your rights, contact the project using section 12. You may also lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority, including the Italian Data Protection Authority (Garante). MathBoard does not use the described analytics for automated decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects.

11. California Privacy Rights

For California residents, under the CCPA: We do not sell your personal information. Board content and preferences remain on your device. Product analytics on the production website and in installed desktop builds is limited as described in sections 2.2 and 2.3.
